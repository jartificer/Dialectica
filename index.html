DIALECTICA

reason your way through 2,500 yearsAdmin mode

Every great thinker has a conviction they would die for. Your task is to talk each of them out of it.

Write to them in plain English. They will answer, push back, try to sidetrack you, and lose patience if you waffle. Find the weak joint in their view, follow the argument where it leads, and bring them to accept the idea they set out to reject.

ConvictionRises each time you land a real argument. Fill it to win.
PatienceDrops when you ramble, repeat yourself, fire off lists of keywords, or walk into an old objection they've already answered.
HintsFree with the early thinkers. Later ones charge a little patience.
DifficultyRises through history. Later thinkers need longer chains of argument.

⚠Content notice. These conversations take on mature subjects, as the philosophers themselves did: death and grief, war and violence, slavery, religion, suffering and despair. Dialectica is meant for adults and is not intended for unsupervised minors. You can stop at any time.

Smart understanding downloads a small language model (about 23 MB, once) so thinkers recognise your argument in your own words. It only reads what you write: every reply is still the thinker's own. Without it, the game understands keywords and everyday remarks.

Admin mode opens every thinker and adds debug tools to each conversation. Wins in admin mode are not saved, so your real progress stays as it is.
